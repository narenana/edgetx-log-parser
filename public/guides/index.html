RC flight log guides

Short, practical how-tos for recording and reading RC flight logs — then replay any of them in 3D with the RC Log Viewer.
  • How to analyze a drone crash log
    Analyze a drone crash log in your browser: scrub to impact, compare RC inputs vs attitude, check battery sag, RSSI loss and failsafe. Free, private.
  • How to enable SD card logging on EdgeTX (and OpenTX)
    Enable SD-card telemetry logging on EdgeTX/OpenTX: add an "SD Logs" Special Function on a switch, set a log rate, then copy the CSV from /LOGS.
  • How to enable Blackbox in Betaflight and download your log
    Enable Betaflight Blackbox in the Configurator: pick a logging device (flash/SD), set a rate, arm and fly, then download the .bbl and read the charts.
  • How to Enable Blackbox in iNAV Configurator
    Enable Blackbox in iNAV Configurator: open the Blackbox tab, set the device to onboard flash or SD, pick a rate, fly and export the log to view in 3D.
  • How to read a blackbox log: a beginner's guide
    How to read a blackbox log: what it records (gyro, PID, RC, motors, battery, GPS), how sample rate works, and how to read one on synced charts.
